Night gate of a waterfront repair depot. A wide open gate, a freestanding kiosk with one blank glowing screen, long rows of closed metal lockers, several small open repair carts parked in neat lines. Concrete yard, glass sheds, warm work lights, cold cyan from the blank screen. No people, no letters, no logos, no trucks. Sharp ink line, no blur. The gate and the kiosk sit in the left two thirds; the right side is a quieter row of lockers.
