A wide night view of a near-future island district in a bay, seen from the water. Clusters of glass towers with warm amber windows, blank cyan screens with no letters, low bridges, smooth driverless pods on a shore road, a few boxy delivery drones in the deep blue sky. Black water in the foreground with long reflections. No people close to the camera, no letters, no logos, no map. Sharp ink line, no blur. The towers fill the left two thirds; the right side is open water and sky.
