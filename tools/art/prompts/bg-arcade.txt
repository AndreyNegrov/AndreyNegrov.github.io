A covered shopping arcade at night, a long glass passage between towers. At the far end, a wide building face carries a running ribbon of blank glowing cyan light, no letters and no numbers. Warm shop windows, polished floor reflecting the lights, a few empty benches. No people. Sharp ink line, no blur, no letters, no logos. The passage and the glowing ribbon sit in the left two thirds; the right side is a calmer wall of glass.
