Night roof of a residential tower above a coastal island district. A slim beacon tower with a dark lamp, a wide view of glass towers and black water, a low bridge far to the right still level with the road. Balconies on the nearer towers hold tiny distant figures, faces not readable. Warm amber windows, deep blue sky, no letters, no logos. Sharp ink line, no blur. The beacon and the island sit in the left two thirds; the right side is open sky and water.
