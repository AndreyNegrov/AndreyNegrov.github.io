Night view from the back seat of a driverless taxi, no steering wheel and no driver, looking forward through a broad clear windshield onto a wet avenue in a near-future coastal district. Glass towers, blank glowing window-screens with no letters and no numbers, dark traffic lights, several low driverless cars stopped in the lanes, boxy delivery drones hanging still in the blue night air. Warm amber light in the windows, cold cyan glow from the screens, reflections on wet asphalt. Crisp sharp edges, no blur, no people, no readable signs, no logos. The avenue and the towers sit in the left two thirds; the right side is quieter glass and sky.
