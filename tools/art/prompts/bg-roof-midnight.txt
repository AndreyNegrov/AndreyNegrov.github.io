The same night roof of a residential tower above a coastal island district. A slim beacon, a wide view of glass towers whose every window is lit warm amber, black water, and the far bridge raised open like two lifted arms, cars stopped before it. Tiny distant figures on balconies, faces not readable. Deep blue midnight sky, no letters, no logos. Sharp ink line, no blur. The beacon and the island sit in the left two thirds; the raised bridge is far and small on the right.
