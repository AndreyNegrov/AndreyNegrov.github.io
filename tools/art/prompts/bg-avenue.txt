Night avenue in a near-future coastal district. Smooth low driverless pods stand still in the lanes, boxy delivery drones hang motionless in the blue air, traffic signals are dark. Wet asphalt, glass towers, blank glowing window-screens with no letters, warm amber windows, cold cyan light. No people. Sharp ink line, no blur, no letters, no logos. The stopped traffic and the towers fill the left two thirds; the right side is a quieter facade.
